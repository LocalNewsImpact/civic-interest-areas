Where Audrain, Boone and Osage residents go

Approximate trips a week by residents of Audrain, Boone and Osage counties into neighbouring counties. Arrow width is trips a week: the dark band is commuting to work, the light band all other trips (shopping, school, medical visits, leisure). Flows of 2,000 trips a week or more.

Sources: visits, GeoDS Lab mobility flows from SafeGraph phone data, weekly averages for 2021 (Kang et al., Scientific Data, 2020); commuting, U.S. Census Bureau LEHD Origin-Destination Employment Statistics (LODES 8), 2023; hospital stays, Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services Hospital Service Area File, 2023 (Medicare inpatients only); population, U.S. Census Bureau American Community Survey 2020-2024 five-year estimates. A schematic: the sources cover different years, visits count each person once per place per week, and all figures are rounded.
