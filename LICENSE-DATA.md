that were made by this project -- are licensed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Credit: Missouri School of Journalism analysis.

Public data from other sources keeps its own terms; each is cited in
[CITATIONS.md](CITATIONS.md). The stories the story data points to remain their
publishers' copyright; no article text is published here. Licensed data (Advan,
through Dewey Data) is not in this repository and is not covered by this licence.
